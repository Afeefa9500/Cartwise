# CartWise review-sentiment model: training report

Generated by `python ml/train.py`. Every number below is measured on **real, human-labelled** review sentences
that the model did not train on (cross-dataset tests) or on whole held-out reviews (grouped CV).

## Data

| Source | Sentences used | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hu & Liu (2004) customer reviews | 3772 | 14 electronics products, sentence label = sign of annotated feature opinions |
| Täckström & McDonald (2011) | 2201 | pos/neg sentences, books/dvds/electronics/music/games |
| Hand-written shopping examples | 218 | delivery, packaging, fit, fabric, skin feel, seller, refunds (`ml/seed_reviews.tsv`) |
| Pang & Lee (2005) movie snippets | 0 | tested, did not help held-out accuracy, so not used |

## Strongest learned features

Positive: `__LP__`, `__VP2__`, `__VP1__`, `good`, `great`, `and`, `price`, `features`, `camera`, `cool`, `simple`, `i_have`, `i_like`, `easy_to`, `phone`

Negative: `__LN__`, `__VN1__`, `only`, `not`, `__VN2__`, `support`, `away`, `would`, `like`, `does_not`, `should`, `don't`, `quiet`, `when`, `would_have`

## Limits

* Binary (positive / negative). Probabilities between 0.40 and 0.60 are reported as *mixed / neutral* by the app.
* Training data is English product reviews (mostly electronics) plus a small hand-written shopping set; accuracy on fashion, beauty or Hinglish text will be lower than the electronics numbers above.
* Sarcasm and long multi-topic reviews are hard; the app therefore scores sentence by sentence.
* Trained in 17s.
